title: "Drag and Drop"
description: "Reschedule, unschedule or delete tasks by dragging them on the calendar."
---

Drag any task card to change its due date. This works in month view and week view, and for sub-tasks as well as regular tasks.

## Move a task to another day

<video

></video>

1. Click and drag a task card.
2. Drop it on a different day. The day you're hovering over gets a blue outline.
3. LinCal updates the due date in Linear right away.

### Notes

- Only the due date changes.
- If **Show weekends** is off, weekend days are hidden and can't be drop targets. See [Display Options](/using-lincal/display-options).

## Schedule a task without a due date

Tasks without a due date sit in the bar at the bottom of the calendar.

1. Expand the bar if you want to browse the tasks.
2. Drag a task card onto a day in the calendar.
3. The task gets that day as its due date.

## Remove a due date

![Drop zones while dragging](/images/using-lincal/LinCal-DragDropZones.png)

1. Start dragging a task card. Two drop zones appear in the bottom bar.
2. Drop the card on **Drop to remove due date**.
3. The task now has no due date and moves into the bar.

You don't need to expand the bar first. Use this when a task should stay unscheduled.

## Delete a task

1. Start dragging a task card.
2. Drop it on the **Delete** zone with the trash icon.
3. The task is moved to Linear's trash, and a **Task moved to trash** message appears.

Changed your mind? Click **View issue** in the message to open the task in Linear, where you can restore it.

## Tips

- Use **Today** or **T** to jump back to the current week before dropping.
- Overdue tasks are quick to reschedule: drag them to a new day.
- Dragging a sub-task changes only that sub-task's due date, not its parent's. To see sub-tasks on the calendar, turn on **Show sub-tasks** in [Display Options](/using-lincal/display-options).
