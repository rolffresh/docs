This page explains how LinCal decides which tasks to load and display, and why some tasks might not appear.

## What gets loaded

### Calendar (tasks with due dates)

- Tasks are loaded **per calendar month** as you scroll.
- Which teams are included depends on your filters:
  - **No filters:** tasks from your own teams.
  - **One or more teams selected:** tasks from those teams.
  - **A [Linear view](/using-lincal/views) selected:** tasks from that view, from any team you have access to.
  - **An assignee, project or label selected, but no team:** matching tasks from any team you have access to.
- All matching tasks for that month are loaded.

### Tasks without a due date (bottom bar)

- LinCal loads up to **100** tasks without a due date, **most recently updated first**.
- Only tasks **updated in the last 2 months** are included.
- The same filters apply as for the calendar. If the list reaches 100, the count shows **100+**. Use filters to narrow it down.

#### Searching the bar

- **Search box:** type to filter the tasks that are already loaded.
- **Search entire workspace:** when the task you want isn't in the loaded list, click this to search task titles beyond it, including tasks not updated in the last 2 months. Results are limited to **100**.

See [Tasks without a due date](/using-lincal/tasks-without-due-date) for more on using the bar.

## Why a task might not show up

1. **Team scope:** with no filters you only see tasks from teams you're a member of. Select more teams in the filter to see others. Selecting an assignee, project, label or view can also bring in tasks from teams you haven't selected.
2. **Display filters:** status, project, assignee, cycle or label filters can hide tasks that match the load criteria.
3. **Completed and sub-tasks:** if **Show completed** or **Show sub-tasks** is off, those tasks are hidden. See [Display Options](/using-lincal/display-options).
4. **Tasks without a due date:** the bar shows at most 100 tasks updated in the last 2 months. For anything older or beyond the 100, use **Search entire workspace**.
5. **Per-day limit:** each day shows up to 4 tasks, and the rest are under the **More** button.
6. **Weekends:** if **Show weekends** is off, weekend days are hidden.
