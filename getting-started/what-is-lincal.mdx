title: "What is LinCal?"
description: "Learn what LinCal is and how it helps you plan your Linear work on a calendar."
---

LinCal puts your Linear work on a calendar. See work laid out by due date in a month or week view, reschedule it by dragging, and create or edit tasks without switching between list views and issue details.

## What you can do with LinCal

- **See your work by due date** in [month or week view](/using-lincal/calendar-view), and find tasks that don't have a date yet in the [bar at the bottom](/using-lincal/tasks-without-due-date).
- **Filter** by team, project, status, assignee, labels, cycles and your saved [Linear views](/using-lincal/views). Reuse combinations with [Recents and Favorites](/using-lincal/recentsfavorites).
- **Reschedule by dragging.** [Drag and drop](/using-lincal/drag-and-drop) a task to a new day, remove its due date, or delete it.
- **Create and edit tasks** from the calendar, including [sub-tasks](/using-lincal/creating-issues), and change status, assignee, project, labels and cycle from the task card.
- **See projects and cycles** on the calendar, with [project start and target dates](/using-lincal/projects-on-calendar) and [cycle starts](/using-lincal/cycles-on-calendar).
- **Choose what you see** with [Display Options](/using-lincal/display-options): card detail, card properties, ordering, and whether to show sub-tasks, completed tasks and weekends.
- **Work from the keyboard** with [shortcuts](/using-lincal/keyboard-shortcuts) for navigation, filters and task creation.
- **Share your current view** with teammates through a [share link](/using-lincal/share-calendar-view).

## How LinCal works with Linear

LinCal is built on top of your Linear data and doesn't need a separate account. You sign in with Linear, and when you create, edit, move or update tasks in LinCal, those changes sync back to Linear. See [Sync Behavior](/using-lincal/sync-behavior) for when data refreshes.
